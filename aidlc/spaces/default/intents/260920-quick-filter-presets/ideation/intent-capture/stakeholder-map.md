# Stakeholder Map — Quick Filter Presets

## Key Stakeholders and Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Decision-maker / Influencer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| The requester (product owner) | Wants the feature to ship correctly, matching the described flow (hardcoded presets + custom, saved filters) | Decision-maker | [Q5] [Q6] |
| End users of the servers catalog | Care about ease of use — one-click access to common views instead of manually rebuilding filters | Influencer (their usage patterns, tracked via analytics, will influence which presets are kept/added) | [Q5] [Q3] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Decision-maker**: The requester is the sole decision-maker for scope and priority on this piece of work. [Q6]
- **Influencers**: End users, indirectly, through observed usage of filters/presets once analytics are in place. [Q5] [Q3]
