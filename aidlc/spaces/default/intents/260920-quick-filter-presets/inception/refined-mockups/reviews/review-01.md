### Summary

The refined mockups are thorough and implementable: every story group (presets, custom-filter CRUD, persistence/URL, analytics, selected-state) has a corresponding UI state across the four documents, the preset→Filter/Sort mapping is technically correct (uptime90 as a 0–1 fraction, clearnet as the exclusive three-marker set, tor as the inclusive single-marker set), and the accessibility checklist adequately covers WCAG 2.1 AA and NFR4 (keyboard operability, ARIA pressed-state, contrast, target size). The one Major finding (R-01) flags a real implementability risk around AC1.1.6's "single coherent view transition" requirement, which the interaction spec's two-sequential-setter-call description doesn't yet demonstrate satisfying — worth resolving before Construction to avoid a double URL push. The remaining findings are minor documentation-consistency gaps (a label mismatch, a couple of under-specified dialog mocks, and a lingering "to confirm" vs. "confirmed" discrepancy inherited from an earlier stage) that a human reviewer should weigh but that don't block moving forward.
